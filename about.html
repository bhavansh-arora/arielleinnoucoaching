About Arielle

Welcome, I am
Arielle Innou
Nahmias.

I am a dedicated life coach who empowers individuals and leaders to navigate significant personal and professional transitions with clarity, compassion, and courage to embrace change.

Who I help

I specialize in supporting women in midlife seeking to rediscover their identity, and leaders striving to cultivate a more harmonious and productive workplace.

If you’ve landed here, it’s possible that life feels particularly demanding right now, a little heavier than usual. Perhaps years of prioritizing the needs of others, your family, your work, and your relationships have led to a feeling of disconnection from your own essential needs.

Whether the desire for change stems from within your inner world or your professional environment, you are seeking more than superficial adjustments. You desire genuine peace, clear direction, and a profound sense of self, achieved without the weight of guilt, the disruption of chaos, or the hindrance of self-doubt.

I understand this journey intimately, having walked this path myself.

My story

My story is one
of resilience.

01Where it began

Fez, Morocco

Growing up as part of a minority community in Fez, Morocco, I experienced firsthand the power of identity and the pain of being misunderstood, navigating environments where freedom of expression was limited and daily life presented unique challenges.

02Boarding school, young

France

My early experiences included attending boarding school in France at a young age…

03A new home

Canada

…and later immigrating to Canada. Together, they instilled in me a resilience and the ability to thrive in diverse settings.

04The entrepreneur

Fashion & real estate

My professional path has been equally varied, from entrepreneurship in fashion and real estate…

05The corporate leader

25 years in healthcare

…to over two decades as a corporate leader in the dental industry, where I excelled in management, mentorship, and business development.

06The calling

Life coaching

Throughout it all, my true calling has been to empower individuals to find their voice, resolve inner conflicts, and feel truly seen. This passion led me to life coaching.

Grounded in the belief that prioritizing emotional well-being and self-care are learned skills, crucial for both personal fulfillment and thriving workplaces.
