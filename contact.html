How does the free 30-minute call work?

It’s a complimentary, no-pressure conversation. We talk about where you are, where you want to go, and whether our approaches align. If we’re a good fit, we’ll map out next steps together.

Do you coach online or in person?

Both. Private 1:1 sessions run via Zoom or in person in Toronto, so you can choose what feels right for you.

Who do you work with?

Women in midlife and beyond (often 50+) who want to reclaim their identity, rewrite old stories and build boundaries that protect their peace—and leaders and teams, especially in dental offices, healthcare and small-to-mid-size businesses, who want a healthier, more productive workplace.

What is a Clarity Plan?

A custom plan we create together after our first conversation. It’s designed to break old patterns and set new boundaries, based on your goals and challenges.

Where are the retreats held?

In the Ontario countryside at Bella Estate Gardens: a beautiful sanctuary of lush gardens, nestled in tranquility away from the busy city.
