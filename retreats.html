The retreat

Complementing my coaching services, I offer a unique opportunity for individuals and groups to step away from the demands of daily life and immerse themselves in a safe, nurturing, and supportive environment.

Bella Estate Gardens

The healing
power of nature.

Experience the healing power of nature in a beautiful retreat sanctuary surrounded by lush gardens and the inspiring presence of the natural world, nestled in magnificent tranquility away from the busy city.

Guests will feel truly nurtured by our dedicated and supportive team, thoughtfully prepared healthy and delicious meals with diverse options, and a range of restorative activities such as yoga, meditation, nature immersion, individualized coaching sessions, insightful workshops, and activities designed to support personal growth and well-being.
